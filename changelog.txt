Version: 1.0.16
Date: 11. 10. 2026
  Changes:
    - Removed the deprecated, hidden "Recycling amount multiplier" setting. Recycling recipes are
      now targeted via a "recycling=<multiplier>" entry in "Item types" and/or the "Recycling
      recipes inherit their source item's multiplier" setting. The old setting's stored value (if
      any) is simply ignored from now on.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.15
Date: 11. 10. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed "Value (109091) outside of range" crash when loading recipes whose scaled
      `ignored_by_stats` / `ignored_by_productivity` values exceeded the engine limit of 65535
      (e.g. dry-ice-recycling with a large multiplier). Those stat values are now clamped to
      the same limit as the product/ingredient counts.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.14
Date: 11. 10. 2026
  Features:
    - New startup setting "Apply minimum crafting time to explicit recipe-category entries"
      (default on): recipes explicitly claimed via a recipe-category entry in "Item types"
      (e.g. "recycling=10") are no longer exempt from the minimum crafting time — the time-based
      minimum can raise their effective multiplier above the category's stated value. When off,
      all explicit "name=number" entries remain fully exempt as before.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.13
Date: 11. 10. 2026
  Features:
    - "Item types" entries can now name recipe categories: an entry like "recycling" (optionally
      "recycling=<multiplier>") scales every recipe whose categories include it. This replaces the
      dedicated recycling multiplier as the way to target all recycling recipes (the engine gives
      every recycling recipe the "recycling" category; modded ones may use e.g. "macerating").
    - New startup setting "Recycling recipes inherit their source item's multiplier" (default on):
      when a recycling recipe's source item (e.g. "iron-plate" for "iron-plate-recycling") was scaled
      with an explicit "name=number" entry, the recycling recipe inherits that multiplier.
  Changes:
    - The "Recycling amount multiplier" setting is deprecated and now hidden. Existing installs keep
      their stored value, which still applies when it differs from the default (10) and no explicit
      claim wins; a fresh install's default no longer forces recycling recipes away from the normal
      criteria. An explicit claim on the recycling recipe itself (e.g. "recycling=15" or
      "iron-plate-recycling=20") always wins.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.12
Date: 02. 10. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Recipes whose products use `extra_count_fraction` (engine-generated recycling recipes such as
      iron-gear-wheel-recycling: 0 plates with a 50% chance of 1 extra) no longer lose their output
      when scaled: the scaled chance's integer part is now folded into the product counts as
      guaranteed output and only a fractional remainder stays as the chance.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.11
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Criteria no longer stack: each recipe is now scaled exactly once, using the highest-priority
      claim. Previously, a recipe matched by both Item types (global multiplier) and Recipe sub
      groups had both multipliers applied on top of each other (e.g. 10 × 30 = 300), which could
      overflow recipe counts beyond the engine limit of 65535.
    - An explicit "subgroup=number" entry now replaces the implicit multiplier of another criterion
      instead of stacking; an explicit claim always wins over implicit ones, and implicit claims
      keep the old criterion priority (Item types → Item names → Sub groups).

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.10
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - Sub group entries in "Recipe sub groups" that match no item subgroup and no recipe's subgroup
      now produce a warning in the startup log (and the in-game startup broadcast), pointing out
      that this setting matches sub groups — explicit per-name multipliers belong in "Item/Recipe names".

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.9
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - New startup setting "Minimum crafting time" (default 0 = off): recipes targeted by the Item
      type / Item name / Sub group criteria are scaled with an integer multiplier of at least
      ceil(minimum / crafting time), so no batch finishes faster than the configured duration.
      Example: a 3.2 s recipe with a 30 s minimum is scaled by ceil(30 / 3.2) = 10 → 32 s batch.
      When the configured multiplier already reaches the minimum, the bigger of the two is used;
      recipes with an explicit "name=number" multiplier are exempt.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.8
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - "Convert to expected value" mode now also handles products whose chance is declared as
      `independent_probability` (e.g. oxide-asteroid-crushing: 30% for an extra oxide chunk).
      Previously those fell through to plain amount multiplication (30% chance of 30 chunks
      with multiplier 30); they now correctly split into 9 guaranteed + 30% chance of 1 extra.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.7
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - New setting "Chance-based product handling" with two modes:
      "Multiply the output amount" (default, previous behaviour): chance stays the same, the output
      amount is multiplied (30% chance of 1 with multiplier 11 -> 30% chance of 11).
      "Convert to expected value": the entry is split so the expected output stays exactly
      proportional (30% chance of 1 with multiplier 11 -> guaranteed 3 + 30% chance of 1 extra).
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a crash on startup when the in-game startup message broadcast exceeded the engine's
      limit of 20 localised string parameters (heavily modded games produce thousands of skip
      messages). The broadcast is now capped at 15 messages and summarizes the remainder, which
      is always written in full to factorio-current.log.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.6
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Added optional dependency on Stack Overload (ezo-stack-overload) so it always runs before this
      mod in the data stage. Previously the load order between the two mods was undefined, so recipes
      could be treated as unbatchable based on stack sizes before Stack Overload raised them.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.5
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - New setting "Scale recipes with mixed stackability products" (default on): when on, recipes
      producing a mix of stackable and non-stackable items (e.g. oxide-asteroid-crushing) are scaled;
      non-stackable product entries keep their original count (max 1 per craft). When off, such recipes
      are skipped entirely (the previous conservative behaviour). Recipes whose products are ALL
      non-stackable are always skipped.
    - Notable startup messages (warnings, skipped recipes, errors) are now broadcast in the chat after
      a map is loaded (data-stage log output is otherwise only visible in factorio-current.log).
    - New console command /recipe-multiplier-startup-log to re-print those messages on demand.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.4
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Recipes with a mix of stackable and non-stackable item products (e.g. oxide-asteroid-crushing:
      ice + oxide-asteroid-chunk) are now scaled correctly instead of being skipped entirely.
      Non-stackable product entries keep their original max count of 1, the rest of the recipe is
      scaled as usual. Only recipes whose products are ALL non-stackable (e.g. asteroid chunk
      upcycling) are still skipped.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.3
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Changes:
    - Fail-safe settings handling: malformed entries in the Item types / Item names / Sub groups
      string settings no longer cause errors. Unparseable entries (or entries with a non-numeric
      multiplier) are skipped with a log message while the remaining valid entries still apply.
    - Non-existing items, recipes, types and sub groups in these settings only produce log
      messages, never errors.
    - Non-numeric multiplier settings fall back to their default value with a log message.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.2
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - New setting "Apply multiplier to Recipe sub groups": target recipes by their sub group id
      (e.g. "crafting", "smelting", "chemistry"), with optional per-group multipliers
      ("smelting=20, chemistry"), working the same way as the Item types and Item names settings.

Version: 1.0.1
Date: 25. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed crash on load: "Recipe item-product X is not stackable but has a max count of N".
      Recipes whose products or ingredients are non-stackable items (stack_size of 1 or the
      "not-stackable" flag, e.g. selection tools like tile-upgrade-planner) are now detected and
      left unscaled instead of being multiplied, since they cannot be batched.
    - Fixed "Add scaled recipe copies" mode: scaled ingredients/products were written to the
      original recipe while the crafting time was scaled on the copy; both are now scaled on the
      copy, leaving the original recipe untouched.
    - Removed a leftover debug error() call that crashed loading when a "tree-killer-recycling"
      recipe was present.
