Version: 1.0.5
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - New setting "Scale recipes with mixed stackability products" (default on): when on, recipes
      producing a mix of stackable and non-stackable items (e.g. oxide-asteroid-crushing) are scaled;
      non-stackable product entries keep their original count (max 1 per craft). When off, such recipes
      are skipped entirely (the previous conservative behaviour). Recipes whose products are ALL
      non-stackable are always skipped.
    - Notable startup messages (warnings, skipped recipes, errors) are now broadcast in the chat after
      a map is loaded (data-stage log output is otherwise only visible in factorio-current.log).
    - New console command /recipe-multiplier-startup-log to re-print those messages on demand.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.4
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Recipes with a mix of stackable and non-stackable item products (e.g. oxide-asteroid-crushing:
      ice + oxide-asteroid-chunk) are now scaled correctly instead of being skipped entirely.
      Non-stackable product entries keep their original max count of 1, the rest of the recipe is
      scaled as usual. Only recipes whose products are ALL non-stackable (e.g. asteroid chunk
      upcycling) are still skipped.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.3
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Changes:
    - Fail-safe settings handling: malformed entries in the Item types / Item names / Sub groups
      string settings no longer cause errors. Unparseable entries (or entries with a non-numeric
      multiplier) are skipped with a log message while the remaining valid entries still apply.
    - Non-existing items, recipes, types and sub groups in these settings only produce log
      messages, never errors.
    - Non-numeric multiplier settings fall back to their default value with a log message.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.2
Date: 26. 09. 2026
  Features:
    - New setting "Apply multiplier to Recipe sub groups": target recipes by their sub group id
      (e.g. "crafting", "smelting", "chemistry"), with optional per-group multipliers
      ("smelting=20, chemistry"), working the same way as the Item types and Item names settings.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.1
Date: 25. 09. 2026
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed crash on load: "Recipe item-product X is not stackable but has a max count of N".
      Recipes whose products or ingredients are non-stackable items (stack_size of 1 or the
      "not-stackable" flag, e.g. selection tools like tile-upgrade-planner) are now detected and
      left unscaled instead of being multiplied, since they cannot be batched.
    - Fixed "Add scaled recipe copies" mode: scaled ingredients/products were written to the
      original recipe while the crafting time was scaled on the copy; both are now scaled on the
      copy, leaving the original recipe untouched.
    - Removed a leftover debug error() call that crashed loading when a "tree-killer-recycling"
      recipe was present.
